# Gathering materials

Good copy comes from good materials. Before writing, collect these from the brief, photos, notes, or conversation. If one can't be found, put a bracket in its place. Never invent it.

## Minimum materials

| Material | Why |
|---|---|
| Exact name | The opening of the text |
| Place | Origin and reason |
| One concrete detail | The center of the text |
| Reader and where they read | Tone and length |
| What the reader should do | The ending |

## Questions that draw out the story

Ask someone who knows the thing. One answer can be enough to bring the text to life. The [question gate](intake.md) decides when to ask and how many questions. This section is only the question bank.

About the thing:

- Where does its name come from?
- When did people make it, and for what?
- Which fact about it do most people not know?
- Is it eaten or used alongside something else?
- What ruins it?

About the work:

- Which step takes the longest?
- Which trick does only the maker know?
- What sound, smell, or feel does it have?
- What exactly do the hands do?

About the person:

- When does a working day start?
- Which tool do they own the most of?
- Who taught them the craft? Only if they said so themselves and agreed to share it.

About use:

- What should you do with it first?
- How does it get better?
- Where should it be kept?

In a Persian conversation, ask the same questions in this wording:

**درباره‌ی چیز**
- اسمش از کجا آمده؟
- کِی و برای چه درستش می‌کردند؟
- چه چیزی را آدم‌ها معمولاً درباره‌اش نمی‌دانند؟
- کنارِ چه چیزی خورده یا استفاده می‌شود؟
- چه چیزی خرابش می‌کند؟

**درباره‌ی کار**
- کدام مرحله از همه وقت‌گیرتر است؟
- فوتِ کوزه‌گری‌اش چیست؟
- چه صدایی، بویی یا حسی دارد؟
- دست‌ها دقیقاً چه می‌کنند؟

**درباره‌ی آدم**
- یک روزِ کاری از کِی شروع می‌شود؟
- چه ابزاری را از همه بیشتر دارد؟
- کار را از چه کسی یاد گرفته؟ فقط اگر خودش گفته و اجازه داده.

**درباره‌ی مصرف**
- اولین کاری که باید با آن کرد چیست؟
- چطور بهتر می‌شود؟
- کجا نگه‌داری می‌شود؟

## Detail ladder

Go from the top down until you reach the rung you actually have:

| Rung | Example (fa) | Example (en) |
|---|---|---|
| Abstract | «باکیفیت» | "high quality" |
| Category | «شیرینیِ محلی» | "a local sweet" |
| Name | «قره حالوا» | "qara halva" |
| Place and time | «اردبیل، زمستان» | "Ardabil, winter" |
| Person and work | «خانوم‌های عشایر در خانه می‌پختن» | "nomad women cooked it at home" |
| Sense and moment | «[بو یا گرما وقتی گرمش می‌کنی]» | "[the smell or warmth when you heat it]" |

The text must get down to the "person and work" rung at least. If the materials don't reach that far, say in the internal note what is missing.

## Working from photos

If all you have is a photo:

- You can write what can be seen: color, dish, light, fabric, hands.
- You can't write what can't be seen: place, year, a person's name, taste.
- Don't describe the photo. Take one detail from it and build the story from the other materials.

## When materials are missing

Write the full text and leave the gaps visible. In Persian:

```
«[اسم]»، [معنای اسم؛ تأیید شود] [جا].
[قصه‌ی کوتاه؛ منبع لازم است.]
```

In English:

```
[name], [confirm: what the name means], [place].
[source needed: short story]
```

Then add a short internal note in the conversation language:

```
برای کامل‌شدن لازم است: معنای اسم، یک جزئیاتِ واقعی از طرزِ تولید.
Needed to finish: what the name means, one real detail of how it is made.
```
